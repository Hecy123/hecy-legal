Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Hecy handles information when you use the paint-by-numbers studio on your iPhone or iPad. Please read it together with the Terms of Use. By using Hecy, you acknowledge this policy.

1. Who we are

Hecy is a consumer creative app that helps you turn a photo into a numbered canvas and color it on your device. In this policy, “Hecy,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the publisher of the Hecy app listed on the App Store product page.

Hecy is designed as a local-first experience. There is no Hecy account, no Hecy sign-in, and no Hecy cloud profile. We do not operate a Hecy server that receives your photos, drafts, notes, or finished canvases for storage or processing.

2. Scope

This policy covers information processed in connection with:

  • installing and using Hecy on an Apple device;
  • optional device permissions such as camera, photos, microphone, speech recognition, and notifications;
  • local project data created inside Hecy;
  • consumable in-app purchases fulfilled through Apple; and
  • optional support notes you write inside the app.

It does not cover third-party websites, Apple services outside the purchase flow described here, or other apps you may use on the same device.

3. Information we process

Hecy processes a limited set of information needed to run the studio on your device.

3.1 Content you create or import

Depending on how you use the app, Hecy may store on the device:

  • photos you capture with the camera or select from your photo library;
  • sample images you choose from the in-app sample library;
  • project titles, optional notes, detail level, resolution, palette size, and custom palette colors;
  • numbered canvas progress, including which areas you have filled;
  • rendered or saved canvas images stored by Hecy;
  • optional support notes you type on the Support screen;
  • appearance preference (light or dark); and
  • onboarding and first-run prompt state so Hecy does not repeat the same prompts unnecessarily.

This content stays in local storage on the phone or tablet where Hecy is installed. Hecy does not upload your photos, notes, or canvases to a Hecy server.

3.2 Canvas Credits and purchase records kept locally

Hecy keeps a local balance of Canvas Credits and a local history of verified pack names, amounts, and Apple transaction identifiers needed to avoid double-crediting the same purchase on this device. These values are stored with your other local app data. They are not used to build a Hecy online account.

3.3 Information we do not collect as an online profile

Hecy does not ask you to create a username or password for Hecy. Hecy does not request your name, email address, phone number, postal address, government ID, payment card number, precise location, contacts list, health data, or browsing history for a Hecy cloud account. Payment card details for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not entered into Hecy forms.

4. How we use information

Information stored by Hecy on the device is used only to:

  • display and edit your projects;
  • generate numbered canvases and apply the colors you choose;
  • remember progress, palette choices, and appearance settings;
  • add Canvas Credits after Apple verifies a consumable purchase;
  • show local support notes you saved for yourself; and
  • honor Sign Out and Clear All Data actions you start in Settings.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your photos or notes for advertising networks, cross-app tracking, or training of third-party generative models.

5. Device permissions

Hecy requests system permissions only when a feature needs them. You can deny a permission and continue using features that do not require it.

5.1 Camera

Camera access is requested only when you tap Take Photo. If you deny access, you can still import from the photo library (if allowed) or use a sample image.

5.2 Photo library

Photo library access is requested when you choose an existing photo or when you save a finished canvas to Photos. If you deny add-only or library access, the matching save or import action stops. Canvases already stored inside Hecy’s local files can remain available in the app.

5.3 Microphone and speech recognition

Microphone and speech recognition are requested only when you start dictation for a title or note. Dictated text is used to fill the field you selected. If you deny these permissions, you can still type. Hecy does not keep a separate cloud transcript service of its own.

5.4 Notifications

The first time you open the studio, Hecy may show the system notification prompt. This version does not schedule local notifications and does not send remote push messages after that prompt. Declining notifications does not block painting, saving, or purchases.

5.5 Tracking

Hecy does not use the App Tracking Transparency prompt to track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising.

6. Sharing and third parties

Because project media and notes stay on the device, Hecy does not share those files with Hecy’s own servers. Limited sharing can occur only in these situations:

  • Apple. In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Apple’s terms and privacy practices apply to payment, receipt validation, and App Store account activity.
  • You. If you save a canvas to Photos or share a file using the system share sheet or another tool you choose, that content leaves Hecy under your control and follows the rules of the destination you pick.
  • Legal or safety requirements. If we are legally required to disclose information we control about the app distribution itself, we will do so only as required by applicable law.

Hecy does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, analytics suites, or social login providers in this version.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until you delete it, clear it in Settings, or remove the app.

  • Sign Out returns you to the welcome screen and keeps local projects, media, notes, credits, and history on the device.
  • Clear All Data permanently removes projects, media, support notes, pack history, and remaining Canvas Credits from this device. This cannot be undone from inside Hecy.
  • Deleting the app generally removes Hecy’s sandbox data for that installation, subject to how iOS manages app storage and any copies you previously saved to Photos or elsewhere.

Support notes are stored only on the device. They are not sent to a support team automatically.

8. Children

Hecy is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children online through a Hecy account, because Hecy does not offer such an account. If you believe a child has provided information to us through App Store channels in a way that requires deletion, contact us using the method in Section 11.

9. Security

We rely on the security of your device, iOS app sandboxing, and Apple’s purchase infrastructure. No method of electronic storage is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your device locked, updated, and protected with a passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, or similar controls. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, Hecy data included in that backup follows Apple’s backup rules.

10. International users

Hecy is distributed through the App Store. Local processing happens on the device where you install the app. If you use Hecy while traveling, the same local-first model applies. Legal rights that cannot be waived under your local law remain available to you.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent through the App Store product page for Hecy (support or developer contact links shown there) or by using the in-app Support screen to keep a local note for your own records. The Support screen does not transmit notes to a remote support desk by itself.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or clearer wording. The effective date at the top will change when we publish an update. Continued use of Hecy after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy. Material changes that require additional consent under applicable law will be handled as required.

13. Summary

In short: your creative work stays on your device; permissions are asked only when needed; purchases go through Apple; and Clear All Data or deleting the app is how you remove local Hecy data.

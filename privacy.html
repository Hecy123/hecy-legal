Privacy Policy

Hecy is a paint-by-numbers studio that keeps your work on this device. Photos you import, drafts, finished canvases, Canvas Credits, pack history, support notes, and appearance choices stay in local storage on the phone or tablet. There is no account, and Hecy does not upload your photos or notes to a Hecy server.

Permissions

Camera access is requested only when you tap Take Photo. Photo library access is requested only when you choose a photo or save a finished canvas. Microphone and speech recognition are requested only when you start dictation for a title or note. If you decline a permission, the matching action stops, and you can still use typing, sample photos, and other features that do not need that permission.

Notifications

The first time you open the studio, Hecy may show the system notification prompt. This version does not schedule or send notifications after that prompt.

Purchases and local data

Purchases are handled by Apple. Verified consumable packs add Canvas Credits on this device. Cancelled, pending, or unverified purchases do not change your balance. Sign Out returns you to the welcome screen and keeps local data. Clear All Data permanently removes projects, media, notes, pack history, and remaining credits from this device.

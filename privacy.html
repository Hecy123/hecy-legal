Privacy Policy

Hecy stores photos, drafts, canvases, pack history, and support notes only on this device. There is no account, and Hecy does not upload your photos to a server.

The camera, photo library, microphone, and speech recognition run only after you tap the matching control. Hecy uses them to take or choose a source photo, save a finished canvas, or turn speech into a title or note.

The first time you open the studio, Hecy shows the system notification prompt. This version does not send notifications.

Purchases are processed by Apple. Canvas Credits and purchase records stay on this device. Removing the app or using Clear All Data deletes that local copy.
